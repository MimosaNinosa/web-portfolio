## The topic

IAB lets a base station carry its own backhaul over the same radio spectrum it uses to serve users, which cuts down on fibre. The trade-off is that traffic can now hop through several wireless relay nodes, and each one has to be trusted.

## What the essay covers

- Attack surfaces specific to multi-hop IAB: interception, spoofing and rogue relay nodes.
- How authentication, handover and spectrum management evolved from 3G to 5G-NR.
- Mitigations for protecting backhaul traffic that passes through untrusted relays.
