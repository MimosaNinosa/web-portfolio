## The problem

Singapore's carparks have tight layouts and strict size limits, and conventional EV chargers aren't designed around them. The result is downtime between cars and less coverage from each charger.

EVAM is an autonomous charger designed for those constraints: hands-free and on demand, so there's less downtime between cars and each unit covers more of the carpark.

## My role

- Technical lead and main coordinator for a team of five, setting the direction of the build.
- Built the life-size prototype, including the robotic arm, from an ESP32, motors, actuators and 3D-printed parts.

## Outcome

- A working life-size model with a functioning robotic arm.
- A full product film rendered in Blender (below).
- The result went beyond what the team and our professors had expected.
